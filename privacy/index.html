Privacy policy

Focus Clock. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Summary

Focus Clock reads your calendar so it can show your meetings on the clock. Your calendar data stays on your clock. We do not run a server that collects, stores or sells your data.

What the clock accesses

  • Google Calendar: read-only access (calendar.readonly) to the events of your calendars: title, start and end time, and whether an event lasts all day.
  • Microsoft Outlook: read-only access to your calendar events (Calendars.Read).
  • iCloud: your calendar events, using an app-specific password that you create and type into the clock.

The clock never creates, changes or deletes events, and does not read email, contacts or files.

Where your data is kept

  • Events are downloaded directly from Google, Microsoft or Apple to your clock over your Wi-Fi, and are kept only in the clock's memory to show today's and upcoming meetings.
  • The sign-in token (or iCloud app-specific password) is stored only on your clock, so it can refresh your events.
  • The sign-in page on this website (/focus-clock-signin/) runs in your phone's browser. It passes Google's one-time sign-in code to your clock on your home network and stores nothing.
  • Your calendar data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

Software updates

The clock checks this website about once a day for a new firmware version. That request does not include your calendar data or account details.

Removing access

  • On the clock: Settings > Calendar (press to disconnect), or a factory reset (hold the knob for 10 seconds), deletes the stored sign-in and events from the clock.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove "Focus Clock".
  • Microsoft: account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Apple: revoke the app-specific password at account.apple.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Focus Clock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
