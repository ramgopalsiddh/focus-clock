Focus Clock

A desk clock with a focus timer and your day's meetings.

What it does

  • Focus timer: start, pause and reset with a knob and buttons; it keeps a running total of your focus time.
  • Agenda: shows today's meetings from Google Calendar, Outlook or iCloud, and beeps a few minutes before each one.
  • Bedside: a dimmed night clock with an alarm.

Connecting your calendar

On the clock, open Agenda and choose your calendar. For Google, scan the QR code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi as the clock and allow read-only access to your calendar. The clock only reads your events to show them; it never changes your calendar.
